⚡ Spark CONCEPT

The public record for ideas — a place to show what you're building, who with, and how. Sharing first is proof of priority, not exposure to theft.

🌳 Original — no parent · 0 forks Started Jun 10, 2026 latest: a3f2c91 · timestamped 14:02 UTC

The problem

People sit on ideas because sharing feels risky, and the thinking behind projects — the orchestration — lives on whiteboards and scattered docs with no public, versioned home. GitHub holds the artifacts; nobody holds the thinking.

The idea

Every idea gets a living page: pitch, story, trials, team, decision log, and a versioned, timestamped history. Publishing openly becomes your receipt. Forks must carry lineage, so attribution is structural. Credit, not exclusivity — multiple people can run at the same idea; the surrounding environment (team, activity, progress) is what differentiates.

Why now

AI is making code the commodity output of ideas. The scarce upstream artifact is intent: prompts, instruction files, roadmaps, decisions. A Spark page packages that as an executable context bundle — fork it, clone it into an agent, and instantiate the project.
