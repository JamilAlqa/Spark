# Vision

Bring a Bring-a-Trailer listing to someone who loves cars and they'll read the comments before the spec sheet. The story is the product; the transaction is downstream of it. Yet every auction platform is built transaction-first — search, filters, countdown clocks — and the storytelling is left to forums and YouTube.

Motive inverts the order. Cars are editorial objects first: provenance, photography, the people and scenes around them. Collections are curated like magazine issues, not generated like search results. The archive is typed and structured so a car's history accumulates instead of evaporating when a listing closes.

The garage makes it personal: the cars you've owned, the ones you follow, the ones you're hunting — a collection of your own that the archive keeps alive.
