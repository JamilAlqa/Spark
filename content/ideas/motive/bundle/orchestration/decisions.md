# Decision log

## 2026-06 — Editorial before transactional

No bidding, no listings in v1. If the stories aren't worth reading without money attached, the thesis is wrong and we should know early.

## 2026-06 — Typed car archive

Every car conforms to a shared type (`types/car.ts` in the repo) instead of free-form posts. Structure is what lets a car's history accumulate across collections and owners.

## 2026-06 — Theme system over single brand look

Collections carry their own palette, the way album art does. The platform chrome stays neutral.
