# Living summary — v12

Merged from 31 accepted positions; 74 contributors credited in history.

## Where the debate stands

**Closest to consensus:** weights are not authored *expression* in the traditional sense — no human can read or intend their contents — but the *training run* is an authored act, like a director's cut of material they didn't film.

**Sharpest open split:** training-data provenance. One camp holds that authorship questions are unanswerable until provenance is auditable; the other that provenance is a licensing question wearing an authorship costume. v12 rewrote this section after the merge of three positions arguing the two questions must be separated to make progress on either.

**Retired in v9:** the "weights are just statistics" position — survived seven versions, retired after the fine-tuning argument showed it proves too much (it would also deny authorship to collage and sampling).
