# Position 031 — merged into v12

**Claim:** provenance and authorship are separable questions, and conflating them stalls both.

**Argument:** whether a training corpus was licensed is a question about *inputs*; whether weights constitute an authored work is a question about the *output's nature*. A fully licensed corpus produces weights exactly as unreadable and unintendable as a pirated one — so licensing cannot be what authorship turns on.

**Effect on summary:** training-data provenance section split in two; rewritten in v12.
