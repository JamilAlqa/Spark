# Moderation norms

1. **Positions, not replies.** A contribution must state a position and its strongest supporting argument. Dunks and dogpiles are declined without prejudice.
2. **Merge on strength, not popularity.** Maintainers merge the arguments that survive scrutiny, including unpopular ones. There are no votes.
3. **Steelman before rebuttal.** A rebuttal that misstates the position it attacks is returned for revision.
4. **Credit is permanent.** Every merged position is credited in the summary's history, even after later versions supersede it.
