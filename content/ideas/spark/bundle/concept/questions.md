# Open questions

- **Naming the unit** — is an idea page a "spark"? Does a fork get its own name ("ember", "branch")?
- **Quality vs. openness** — how does the feed stay alive without upvotes? Current bet: activity-weighting plus lightweight curation.
- **Identity** — do real names have to be required for attribution to mean anything, or do pseudonyms with persistent reputation work?
- **Moderation** — conversations and concepts invite debate; what are the norms, and who holds them?
- **Bundle divergence** — when a fork's bundle and its parent drift far apart, what does a "merge back" mean for prose the way it means something for code?
