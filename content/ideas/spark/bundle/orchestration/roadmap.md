# Roadmap

## Now — prove the object

The smallest version that proves the thesis is the idea page itself:

1. Idea pages with pitch, story, team, and versioned, timestamped history
2. Context bundles with the standard layout, browsable on the page
3. Activity feed (chronological, filterable by type)
4. Profiles auto-built from the attribution graph
5. Comments
6. Forking with mandatory lineage links

## Next

- Structured contributions: propose a diff on a bundle file, team accepts or declines, credit is permanent (PR flow for prose)
- Open roles and applications on idea pages
- "Clone bundle" that actually hands the bundle to an agent

## Later / deferred

- Follow-based feed personalization (needs critical mass first)
- Cryptographic timestamping of versions
- Search and topics taxonomy
