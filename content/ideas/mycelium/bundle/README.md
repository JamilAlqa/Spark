# Mycelium Packaging

Growing compostable shipping foam from agricultural waste.

Polystyrene foam is functionally immortal and functionally unrecyclable. Mycelium grown on agricultural waste can match its cushioning performance and compost in weeks. Every batch — including the failures — is published as a trial with method and data attached. Three labs have forked the protocol to test regional substrates; lineage keeps the results comparable.

This is a research bundle: the weight lives in `execution/` — the protocol and the trial log are the product.
