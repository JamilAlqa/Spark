# Protocol v1.1

1. **Substrate prep** — agricultural waste (rice hull blend per the merged fork revision), pasteurized 90 min at 65 °C, cooled to room temperature.
2. **Inoculation** — Ganoderma lucidum spawn at 10% by dry weight, mixed under clean conditions.
3. **Forming** — packed into molds at target density; 5 days dark growth at 24 °C, 85% RH.
4. **Demold and second growth** — 2 days for surface mycelium to skin over.
5. **Drying** — 48 h at 60 °C to halt growth and stabilize.

Record every batch as `trials/batch-NN.md` with substrate, dates, density, and compression results. Deviations are fine — undocumented deviations are not.
