# Funding models — three trials, one survivor

## Trial 1 — membership (failed)
Sliding-scale membership covered 70% of rent in month one, then collapsed — and worse, members started treating non-members as guests in "their" room. Killed it: it created the exclusion we exist to remove.

## Trial 2 — grants (partial)
A community-foundation grant covered the renovation but cannot cover operations; grant cycles are too slow to pay rent. Useful for capital costs only.

## Trial 3 — patron floor (survivor)
Twenty patrons commit a small monthly amount with no perks, no recognition wall, no status. Covers rent at 85% occupancy of the commitment. The absence of perks is the design: nothing to defend, nothing to gate.
